# Code of conduct

Prava UI should be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background, or identity. This code applies to everyone taking part, in every project space and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions and experience.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Insults, personal attacks, and sexual language or imagery.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting a problem

If something happens that concerns you, please contact the maintainer privately, using the contact details on [@dineshdcshinde's GitHub profile](https://github.com/dineshdcshinde). Reports are treated confidentially, and you won't be penalized for raising a concern in good faith.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews every report and decides what to do, fairly and based on what happened. That can range from a private conversation or a public warning to removing content or banning someone from the project, either temporarily or permanently. The maintainer can remove any comment, commit, or issue that breaks this code.

## Where this comes from

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
